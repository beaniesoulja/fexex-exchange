# Fexex Brand Identity

## The idea

**Value in motion.**

Fexex should make exchanging value feel less like a complicated financial task and more like a clear, confident next move. The identity is calm rather than noisy, energetic rather than urgent, and human rather than overly corporate.

The visual concept is **Signal Forward**: two angled strokes create a compact forward mark. It carries the feeling of an `F`, an exchange route, and a confident arrow without copying any existing exchange brand.

## Brand foundations

| Element | Direction |
| --- | --- |
| Name | **FEXEX** — always uppercase in product, marketing, and editorial use. |
| Tagline | **Value in motion.** |
| Promise | A clearer way to move the value you already have. |
| Personality | Assured, candid, progressive, calm. |
| Voice | Short sentences. Plain language. Show the next step before asking for it. |

### Sample voice

- Trade value. Feel certain.
- Your next move, simplified.
- Clear rates. No noisy guesswork.
- Ready when your card is.

Avoid hype such as “the best,” “guaranteed,” “instant” or unsupported trust and user-count claims.

## Core palette

| Role | Name | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary dark | Ink | `#161818` | Backgrounds, primary type, logo strokes |
| Primary light | Paper | `#F4F3EE` | Main canvas, reversed type |
| Signal | Motion Lime | `#C6F65C` | Primary action, status highlights, mark tile |
| Accent | Soft Lilac | `#D6C7FF` | Editorial moments, illustration fields |
| Quiet | Moss Grey | `#777A75` | Supporting copy and dividers |

Use Motion Lime sparingly: it is a directional signal, not a full-page background.

## Typography

- **Primary:** a sturdy, modern grotesk with high legibility (Sora SemiBold / Bold is the intended character).
- **Editorial accent:** a restrained serif for one or two words at a time (DM Serif Display Regular is the intended character).
- **Numbers and utility:** the primary grotesk, tabular figures enabled for rates and balances.

The contrast is deliberate: clean sans-serif language makes the product feel dependable, while the rare serif moment brings warmth and motion to key statements.

## Logo rules

The logo consists of the forward mark and the custom lowercase wordmark. Use the supplied SVGs as the source of truth.

- Use the black lockup on Paper or Motion Lime.
- Use the reverse lockup on Ink only.
- Keep at least the height of the mark clear on every side.
- Never stretch, outline, add a drop shadow, rotate, or recolour the strokes.
- Use the mark alone only where the Fexex name is already visible or the space is genuinely constrained.

## Graphic system

- **Shapes:** rounded rectangles, circles, and fine orbital lines.
- **Perspective:** small cards can be slightly tilted to imply motion; keep product content level and easy to read.
- **Photography:** if introduced later, use tactile, close-cropped scenes with a single subject and strong light. Avoid generic fintech stock imagery and floating crypto coins.
- **Icons:** simple one-weight line icons with deliberately rounded caps.
- **Motion:** a short forward slide or progress fill—not a bounce. The feeling should be “moving onward,” not “trying to get attention.”

## Rollout order

1. Establish the Fexex logo and palette in social/profile assets.
2. Apply the navigation, CTA, and type styles to the landing page.
3. Bring the trade journey and sign-in experience into the system.
4. Rework dashboard states, notifications, and transaction receipts.

The product remains unchanged until each screen is intentionally approved for application.
